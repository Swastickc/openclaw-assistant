OpenClaw Assistant

A private, single-user personal assistant.

This application is a personal automation tool operated by its owner for their own use. It connects to the owner's Google account to read email, calendar, files and spreadsheets so it can produce summaries, reminders and spending reports for that one person.

It is not a public product, it has no other users, and it does not accept sign-ups.
